## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS with minimal client-side interactivity (contact form via Web3Forms and EN/NL language switching) — no heavy framework, keeping the implementation aligned with the KISS philosophy. Deployed to GitHub Pages (`ErnieBernie10/ErnieBernie10.github.io`) on custom domain `boedt.me`.

## Users

- **Recruiters** evaluating Arne Boedt for freelance roles and looking for a clear summary of experience plus a direct way to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal freelance developer website for Arne Boedt that lets recruiters and prospective clients quickly understand what he builds, verify his background, and reach out directly without marketing fluff or unnecessary friction.

## Product Principles

1. **Practice KISS in form and substance:** Keep the site lean, direct, and free of unnecessary technical or verbal complexity so the site itself demonstrates the engineering philosophy.
2. **Honesty over hype:** Present real skills and experience plainly; never pad with buzzwords, inflated claims, or fabricated social proof.
3. **Respect the visitor's time:** Make it effortless for recruiters and project leads to scan capabilities, verify the track record, and make contact.
4. **Informal, professional, European:** Speak like a grounded, approachable EU-based engineer in both English (primary) and Dutch, without stiff corporate jargon.
